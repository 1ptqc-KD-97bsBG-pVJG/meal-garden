Roasted Chickpea Rice Bowls

Ingredients

Cook

  1. Start the ricePut 200 g brown rice, 450 ml water and 1.5 g salt in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, cover and reduce to low heat. Cook 35 minutes or the package time until tender, then rest covered 5 minutes.
    35:00Keep this page open for timer alerts.
  2. Prepare the trayHeat a conventional oven to 220°C / 425°F. Drain 400 g cooked chickpeas and pat dry. Toss with 400 g broccoli, 15 ml olive oil and 1.5 g salt on a rimmed tray. Spread into one uncrowded layer.
  3. Roast the vegetablesPlace the tray on the middle oven rack. Roast 22 minutes, tossing after 12 minutes. Broccoli should be tender with browned edges; chickpeas should be hot throughout. Give pale or firm florets another 3 minutes.
    22:00Keep this page open for timer alerts.
  4. Build the bowlsFluff the rested rice. Divide rice, chickpeas and broccoli among four bowls, adding 30 ml lemon juice across the bowls. Refrigerate reserved portions in shallow containers within two hours; freeze later portions promptly.
Equipment: stove, medium saucepan with lid, conventional oven, rimmed tray.

Notes

  • Refrigerate within two hours in shallow covered containers. Use within four days or freeze promptly.
  • Heat stored savory portions until steaming throughout. Stir to eliminate cold spots.
