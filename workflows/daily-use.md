# Daily workflows

## Discover and develop a recipe

Search the existing catalog first. For unfamiliar or technique-sensitive dishes, research appropriate sources and retain links and adaptation rationale. Keep source method, tested status and rotation separate. Normalize selected candidates into explicit quantities, executable steps, yield and equipment; validate before marking ready. No automatic household restrictions or portion multipliers.

## Plan and shop

Read the household person's profile, current equipment and known inventory. Choose one or more explicit batches with intended meal dates; do not count planned leftovers as already cooked. Preserve favorite feedback while allowing variety. Aggregate structured ingredient demand and compare compatible units only. Subtract only confirmed usable inventory; show unknowns for checking at home. Preserve exact recipe demand beside store package quantities. Regenerate outputs, then sync the requested rows using the household integration guidance in its docs/samsung-food.md. Check external state before and after each change.

## Cook, substitute and learn

Open the current card for the requested date. Use a new meal-specific variation for temporary substitutions; only change the master recipe when the change is intended to last. After reported cooking, record actual usage and remaining quantities with dates/storage, without inventing yield. Update inventory and feedback, then regenerate affected outputs. Record source method separately from whether the person actually liked the result. At-home shopping checkmarks are not confirmed receipt quantities.

## Audit and extend

Keep current state in data/ and culinary facts in recipes/. generated/ must be rebuildable. Check readiness, recipe IDs/revisions, source hashes, shopping totals and external operation outcomes. Preserve a working daily release while adding receipt/photo observations, freshness rules, notifications or an app. Before unattended or concurrent writes, implement transactional update operations and retry deduplication.
