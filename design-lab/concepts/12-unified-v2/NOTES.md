# Unified prototype

A fictional sample demonstrating the food timeline, grouped kitchen review, recipe amount controls, cook sequencing, portion destinations, capture, receipt drill-down and nutrition ranges. Data and the inline offline fallback are generated examples, not real food or health history.

The scripted recipe and pantry interactions demonstrate interface behavior. They do not replace the production graph or sequencer. Only amount preferences persist locally. Health ranges are illustrative and personal targets remain unset.

Review kitchen switching, discoverability of amount controls, timer transitions and legibility at cooking distance.
